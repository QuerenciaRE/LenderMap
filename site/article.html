One county, five lenders: reading the call reports behind the ranking

Every county has the same handful of lender types. Here is what each one's quarterly filing says about whether it will look at your loan, using a made-up county and rounded, illustrative numbers.

8 minute read · Illustrative figures, not real lenders

The ranking on LenderMapper boils each lender down to six numbers. Read one county from the courthouse outward and the pattern is easy to see: the lenders most likely to say yes are the ones already growing their commercial real estate book, with room left under their limits.

What to do with this

Call the tier A and B lenders in your county first; they are the ones whose filings show them making these loans now. Then work outward to the branches. The score cannot promise a yes, but it does tell you where your first five calls should go.
