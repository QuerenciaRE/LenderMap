The data

Built from the filings no lender can skip

Every insured bank and credit union files a call report each quarter. LenderMapper reads all of them, back to 2016, and turns the commercial real estate lines into a ranking you can check line by line.

Nothing is self-reported to us and nobody pays to rank higher. Sponsored placements are labelled and sit outside the ranked lists.
