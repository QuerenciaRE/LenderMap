Design system

LenderMapper style guide

Olive and terracotta, Heritage type, Comfortable spacing, Stamped components, Press devices and Film imagery. Every value on this page comes from styles/tokens.css.

Color

Small text needs 4.5:1, large text, icons and chart marks 3:1. Small text in the accent hue uses accent-text. On-accent passes only at 24px or bold 18.66px and up.

    Imagery
